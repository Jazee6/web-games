Sudoku is a logic puzzle played on a 9 × 9 grid: fill every empty cell with a digit from 1 to 9 so that each row, each column and each of the nine 3 × 3 boxes contains every digit exactly once, using only the given digits as clues.

## How to Play

1. Pick a cell. Givens (the bold digits) are fixed; every other cell is yours to fill.
2. Enter a digit. A digit may appear only once in its row, its column and its 3 × 3 box.
3. Use notes to keep track of the digits a cell could still hold.
4. The puzzle is solved when all 81 cells are filled correctly. The timer stops and your time is shown.

Every puzzle has exactly one solution and can be solved by logic alone, without guessing.

| Difficulty | What it takes                                                         |
| ---------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Easy       | Hidden singles and naked singles only                                 |
| Medium     | Adds pointing, box/line reduction, naked pairs and hidden pairs       |
| Hard       | Needs at least one of naked triples, hidden triples, X-Wing or Y-Wing |

The **Daily** puzzle is the same for every player on the same day and changes at 00:00 UTC. Its difficulty follows the day of the week:

| Day (UTC)                   | Difficulty |
| --------------------------- | ---------- |
| Monday, Tuesday             | Easy       |
| Wednesday, Thursday, Friday | Medium     |
| Saturday, Sunday            | Hard       |

## Tips

- Start with hidden singles: look at one box, row or column and ask where a missing digit can still go.
- Cells that share a row, column or box with the selected cell are shaded, and every copy of the selected digit is highlighted, so blocked positions are easy to spot.
- Conflicting digits are shown in red with a wavy underline. Fix them before going further.
- Placing a digit removes that digit from the notes of every cell in the same row, column and box.
- The small number on each digit button shows how many copies are still missing. A crossed-out digit is complete.
- A hint first points out any wrong digit. Otherwise it fills one cell that can be deduced and names the technique that finds it.
- On a small screen, **Zoom in** makes every cell large enough to tap. Swipe to move around the grid. The view follows the selected cell.
- Your game is saved as you play. Reload the page and you can continue, undo history included.

## FAQ

### Does every Sudoku puzzle here have only one solution?

Yes. Every puzzle is checked for a unique solution before it is shown, and it can be solved by logic without guessing.

### How is the difficulty of a Sudoku decided?

By the hardest technique a solver needs, not by how many cells are empty. Easy needs only singles, Medium adds pointing, box/line reduction and pairs, and Hard needs triples, X-Wing or Y-Wing at least once.

### When does the daily Sudoku change?

At 00:00 UTC every day. Everyone gets the same puzzle on the same UTC date, and its difficulty depends on the day of the week.

### How do I write notes in Sudoku on a phone?

Long-press a digit below the grid, or turn on Notes and tap digits. Tap the same digit again to remove the note.

### Do hints affect my time?

The timer keeps running while you use hints. Your best time for each difficulty, shown in the New game menu, only counts games finished without hints.

### Can I undo a move in Sudoku?

Yes. Undo and redo work for every move, including hints, and the history is kept even if you reload the page.

### Does the timer stop when I leave the page?

Yes. The timer pauses whenever the game is hidden, for example when you switch tabs, and continues when you return.

### Is this Sudoku free to play?

Yes. It is free, runs in the browser on phones and computers, and needs no account or download.

Web Games is a small collection of free puzzle games that run in your browser. Every game is designed, built and illustrated in-house.

## What you can expect

- No download, no account, no sign-up.
- Every game works with touch on phones and with keyboard and mouse on desktop.
- Progress is saved in your browser.
- New games are added every month.

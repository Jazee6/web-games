This page explains what Web Games stores and collects. It is a draft and will be finalized before launch.

## Game progress

Game progress is stored in your browser's local storage on your device. It is never sent to our servers.

## Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies and does not track you across sites.

## Advertising

The site does not show ads. If that changes, this policy will be updated first.

Every game on this site runs directly in your browser. There is nothing to install and no account to create.

## Your progress

Progress is saved in your browser on the device you play on. Clearing site data removes it.

## Daily Puzzle

Each classic puzzle has a Daily Puzzle. Everyone gets the same puzzle on the same UTC date, so you can compare results with friends.

| Feature | Phone | Desktop |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Controls | Touch | Keyboard and mouse |
| Fullscreen | Yes | Yes |
| Offline | No | No |
